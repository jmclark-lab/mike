Synthetic MSA dispute clause.

"This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflicts principles. The state and federal courts located in New York County shall have exclusive jurisdiction. CRO irrevocably waives any claim that such courts are an inconvenient forum and any right to seek interim relief from courts at the place of performance."

Performance: site management and regulatory-support services in Colombia, Mexico, and Brazil. Payments in USD to a Colombian SAS. No patient records are in the excerpt.
