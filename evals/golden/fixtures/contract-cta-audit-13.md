Synthetic audit clause.

"Sponsor and its designees may audit CRO and any subcontractor at any time, without notice, and may copy any quality, finance, or HR record. CRO shall pay all third-party auditor fees and travel. Findings are Sponsor's confidential information; CRO may not share them with its other clients or its own quality unit without Sponsor consent."

CRO also supports unrelated sponsors. No trial subjects are identified.
