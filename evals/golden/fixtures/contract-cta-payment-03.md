Synthetic Work Order economics for CTA WO-17 between Andes Clinical Partners S.A.S. (CRO) and Sierra Norte Therapeutics B.V. (Sponsor).

Sponsor proposal: "100% of the study-management fee (USD 380,000) is contingent on First Subject In at each of eight sites. No startup, ethics, or import-support fees are payable if a site is selected but never enrolls. Pass-throughs are reimbursed 90 days after Sponsor receives the third-party invoice, and only if the corresponding subject has been enrolled."

Sites are not yet ethics-approved. Import of investigational product typically lags first ethics opinion by several weeks. No patient-level data is included.
