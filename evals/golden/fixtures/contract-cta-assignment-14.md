  Sponsor may freely assign this CTA to any affiliate or acquirer;
  CRO cannot assign even to a wholly-owned affiliate and cannot
  subcontract monitoring. What assignment/subcontract position is
  workable for a LATAM CRO?

rubric: Mutual affiliate assignment; CRO may subcontract with notice while remaining responsible.

Synthetic assignment clause.

"Sponsor may assign this Agreement to any person without CRO's consent. CRO shall not assign this Agreement or subcontract any Services, including monitoring, regulatory publishing, or local depot tasks, without Sponsor's prior written consent, which may be withheld for any reason. Any attempted subcontract is void and an event of default."

CRO's operating model uses qualified regional CRAs and a licensed local importer as a vendor. No PHI is present.
