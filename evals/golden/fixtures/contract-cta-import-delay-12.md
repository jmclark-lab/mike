question: >
  This CTA treats any delay in investigational-product import as a CRO
  breach with liquidated damages, even when the national agency holds
  the shipment. How should force majeure and regulatory delay be drafted?
must_mention:
  - force majeure
  - import
  - agency
must_not_claim:
  - CRO controls customs release
  - this is legal advice
rubric: Agency/customs holds are not CRO breach; use force majeure or an equitable schedule relief.
---

Synthetic delay clause.

"Time is of the essence. If First Subject In occurs more than 30 days after ethics approval, CRO shall pay liquidated damages of USD 15,000 per week. Delays caused by import-permit review, customs inspection, or agency questions about labeling are not excused, because CRO is responsible for 'all regulatory logistics.'"

Investigational product ships from the EU. Labeling must be approved by the national medicines agency before release. CRO prepares the dossier; the agency issues the permit. No patient data is included.
