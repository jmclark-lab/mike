Synthetic confidentiality clause in a CTA between Selva BioLogistics Ltda. (CRO/importer support) and Pampa MedTech S.R.L. (Sponsor).

"CRO shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party, including ethics committees, national medicines agencies, customs authorities, or insurers, without Sponsor's prior written approval on a document-by-document basis. Required-by-law disclosures are allowed only after Sponsor has had 30 business days to seek a protective order, even if the legal deadline is shorter."

The study cannot start without ethics opinion and an import permit for the investigational product. No subject-level data is in this excerpt.
