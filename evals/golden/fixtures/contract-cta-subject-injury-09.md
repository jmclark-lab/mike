  The subject-injury clause makes the CRO pay for all medical care
  related to study procedures, including injuries caused by the
  investigational product or protocol design. How should this be
  reallocated?
must_mention:
  - sponsor
  - investigational product
  - protocol
must_not_claim:
  - CRO pays for product-related injury
  - this is legal advice
rubric: Sponsor typically covers IP- and protocol-related injury; CRO covers only its negligence.

Synthetic medical-care clause.

"CRO shall promptly arrange and pay for all medical care required by a Study subject for any injury, illness, or complication arising from Study procedures, the investigational product, or the Protocol, without regard to fault. Sponsor shall reimburse CRO only if CRO proves the injury was caused solely by Sponsor's willful misconduct."

Open-label oncology supportive-care study. Product is Sponsor-supplied. Protocol is Sponsor-owned. No names, dates of birth, or medical-record numbers appear.
