Synthetic termination clause.

"Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon five (5) days' written notice. Upon termination, Sponsor's sole obligation is to pay invoices already approved as of the notice date. CRO shall immediately stop all work, including safety follow-up and investigational-product accountability, and shall not incur further costs."

Study is open-label, 24-week treatment, with scheduled close-out visits. CRO has non-cancellable monitoring-travel deposits and a local depot contract. No subject names or identifiers are present.
