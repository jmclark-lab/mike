Synthetic insurance schedule, CTA between Andes Clinical Partners S.A.S. (CRO) and Sierra Norte Therapeutics B.V. (Sponsor).

"CRO shall, at its sole cost, maintain (i) clinical trial / subject-injury insurance with limits of not less than USD 20,000,000 per occurrence covering all Study subjects in all countries, naming Sponsor as additional insured and primary; (ii) professional liability of USD 20,000,000; and (iii) product liability for the investigational product. Certificates are a condition precedent to first payment."

The investigational product is Sponsor-manufactured. Sites will be in at least two LATAM jurisdictions that expect the sponsor or legal representative to evidence trial insurance. No subject identifiers are included.
