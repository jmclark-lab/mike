Synthetic CTA IP clause between Costa Pacífico Research, S.A. de C.V. (CRO) and Pampa MedTech S.R.L. (Sponsor).

"All data generated in the Study, including case report forms, laboratory results, and images, shall be jointly owned by the Parties. Each Party may freely commercialize Study inventions without accounting to the other. CRO may publish any Study results immediately upon database lock."

The study is a first-in-country device feasibility study. No subject identifiers appear in this excerpt. CRO's background SOP library and monitoring templates pre-existed the deal.
