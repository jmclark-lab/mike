Synthetic publications clause.

"Sponsor shall have the exclusive right to publish Study results. CRO and any investigator shall not publish, present, or disclose any Study data, including negative results or methodology, at any time during or after the Study, unless Sponsor gives prior written consent, which may be withheld in Sponsor's sole discretion. This restriction survives termination indefinitely."

Multi-center Phase 3, academic sites, ethics committees that expect results communication. No patient-level listings are attached.
