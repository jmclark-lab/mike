Synthetic Master Services Agreement between Lago Azul Site Management Ltda. (CRO) and Atlántico Device Labs S.A. (Sponsor).

Clause 14: "CRO's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the prior 30 days, except that this limitation shall not apply to (a) any claim by Sponsor, (b) confidentiality, (c) IP infringement, (d) data protection, (e) gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (f) indemnification. Sponsor's liability is limited to fees unpaid. Neither Party excludes liability that cannot be limited by applicable law."

The MSA will cover multiple LATAM work orders for monitoring and vendor management. No PHI is attached.
