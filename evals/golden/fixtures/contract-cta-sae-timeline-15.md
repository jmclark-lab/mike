Synthetic safety-reporting clause.

"CRO shall ensure that each site reports every serious adverse event, including those not related to investigational product, to Sponsor's medical monitor by telephone within two (2) hours of the investigator becoming aware, at any hour. Failure to meet this timeline once is a material breach not subject to cure. CRO is solely responsible for SUSAR reporting to all ethics committees and national agencies."

Protocol (synthetic) currently says investigators report SAEs to the sponsor within 24 hours. Expedited agency reporting remains a sponsor or legal-representative duty in the countries contemplated. No subject identifiers are included.
